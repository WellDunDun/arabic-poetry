# Arabic poetic craft: deeper source findings

Read on 2026-10-14. This pass extends the original prosody research with five authored critical works. It reads the relevant passages, rather than relying on search summaries. The recommendations below are an agent workflow derived from those passages; they are not rules quoted from the sources. Historical critical preferences should remain attributed to their authors.

## 1. Treat syntax as a choice of meaning

Abd al-Qahir al-Jurjani, [دلائل الإعجاز, فصل النظم هو توخي معاني الإعراب](https://www.islamweb.com/ar/library/index.php?ID=7&bk_no=239&flag=1&page=bookcontents), displayed printed pp. 55–56, numbered paragraph 48. The passage explains composition through relationships among words: subject and predicate, verb and object, qualification, condition, negation, and interrogation. It locates verbal arrangement in intended meaning.

Practical consequence: a grammar review should examine who acts, what a modifier qualifies, where negation reaches, what a pronoun refers to, and what a condition entails. A revision that fixes case or word order may change the speaker's proposition. Confirm that proposition before rescanning the line. Do not replace a syntactic decision with an ornate synonym merely because the synonym fits the meter.

In [دلائل الإعجاز, تمام القول في النظم وأنه توخي معاني النحو](https://www.islamweb.net/amp/ar/library/content/239/68/%D9%81%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%A3%D9%86%D9%87-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AE%D9%89-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88), displayed printed pp. 370–374, paragraphs 442–447, al-Jurjani examines Abu Tammam's metaphor for a pen's ink. Reversing which noun is predicate reverses the intended comparison, even if the printed word sequence remains unchanged. This supplies a useful warning for interpreting unvocalized poetry: state consequential grammatical alternatives instead of treating the first parsing as established meaning.

The work's argument about Qur'anic inimitability is its historical purpose. This skill can use the linguistic analysis without converting that purpose into a poetry assessment rubric.

## 2. Explain what an image does

Al-Jurjani, [أسرار البلاغة, فصل في مواقع التمثيل وتأثيره](https://mizanalarab.com/bab/21600017), especially the comparison of Abu Tammam's statement about envy with the following image of fire releasing aloeswood's fragrance, and the explanation beginning فأول ذلك وأظهره. The source argues that representation through sensed experience can make an abstract claim felt, reveal its degree, and affect the hearer beyond merely restating it.

Practical consequence: for the strongest or weakest image, identify the compared things, the connection that permits the comparison, and its effect in this passage. Test whether an image changes what the reader perceives or only decorates a familiar declaration. Concrete detail is one available method, not a mandatory replacement for symbolic or abstract writing. Preserve a purposeful unfamiliar image; explain why it works or where its connection fails.

The host identifies this as the 1422 AH Dar al-Kutub al-Ilmiyya text via Shamela, with the editor's introduction and footnotes removed. It is an authored primary text on a modern transcription site, not a facsimile verified against every printed page. Use named sections as locators.

## 3. Sound patterning must earn its place

Al-Jurjani, [أسرار البلاغة, القول في التجنيس](https://mizanalarab.com/bab/21600002), passages beginning فقد تبين لك أن ما يعطي التجنيس and وعلى الجملة. He compares successful and unsuccessful wordplay, then explains that meaning should lead the use of jinas and saj'. He also accepts deliberately sought ornament when its semantic fit makes it feel unforced.

Practical consequence: do not ban repetition, alliteration, parallelism, or wordplay as inherently artificial. Ask what each recurrence contributes: emphasis, irony, a changed meaning, continuity, or rhythm. Replace forced rhyme or jinas when it distorts the intended sense. For prose poetry, identify recurring sounds and sentence patterns and explain their local effect; their presence alone does not establish poetic quality or a Khalilian meter.

This is a critical argument about successful ornament, not a count-based rule specifying how many devices a poem may contain.

## 4. Revision includes construction and transitions

Ibn Rashiq al-Qayrawani, [العمدة في محاسن الشعر وآدابه](https://www.arabic-keyboard.info/books/ar/read-book/العمدة-في-محاسن-الشعر-وآدابه), chapters المطبوع والمصنوع, القطع والطول, and النسيب. The first describes revision through diction, expression of meaning, poetic construction, rhyme, and connected wording. The second discusses the value of short pieces and long poems, and records differing thresholds for calling a work a قصيدة. The third reports al-Hatimi's concern that an opening connect with what follows.

Practical consequence: examine a line in its neighbors as well as alone. Record whether it develops an image, changes the situation, or repeats material already established. Suggest a transition or deletion when the connection fails. Respect a requested مقطوعة or occasional poem; avoid imposing a lengthy narrative arc. Do not classify a piece with a universal seven- or ten-bayt threshold.

The online transcription has imperfect section placement and no consistently displayed edition pagination. These named chapters are the reliable locators for this pass. Its reported historical anecdotes and period judgments are not independently verified facts about every poet. Its restrictions on poetic vocabulary should not become contemporary writing rules.

## 5. Keep unity and artistic freedom as attributed aims

Ahmad Zaki Abu Shadi, [قضايا الشعر المعاصر, دفاع عن الشعر](https://www.safahat.org/books/31462469/1/), especially the discussion following the prose-poem examples and the passage beginning وإذا قدرنا ألوان هذا الشعر. Abu Shadi argues for room for different forms and poetic subjects. This is evidence of an advocate's position, not proof of a universal scholarly consensus. His historical uses of شعر حر، مرسل، منثور should not automatically replace today's operational form labels. Do not adopt his claims of priority for literary innovations without separate historical research.

In [خليل مطران](https://www.safahat.org/books/31462469/8/), the passage beginning دعم مطران وحدة القصيدة discusses poem unity, the artist's personality, and freedom from ornamental constraints. Practical consequence: judge coherence at the scale the work intends, considering image development, shifts in address, and the relation between the opening and ending. Give classical, taf‘ila, and prose forms equal access to this craft review. These are proposed review questions, not a claim that every good poem must maintain a single subject or end in a revelation.

The [publisher's book page](https://www.safahat.org/books/31462469/) verifies the author and states that the original text is public domain. This is Abu Shadi's book, not Nazik al-Mala'ika's identically titled work. This pass does not present it as current taf‘ila theory.

## 6. Recognize a requested form beyond the three common labels

Ibn Sana al-Mulk, [دار الطراز في عمل الموشحات](https://www.arabic-keyboard.info/books/ar/read-book/دار-الطراز-في-عمل-الموشحات), authored مقدمة ابن سناء الملك في نظم الموشحات, subsection حد الموشح and the paragraph on الأقفال والأبيات. He describes repeating aqfal that agree in weight, rhyme, and part counts, alongside abyat with matching weight and part counts but differing rhymes. His examples distinguish openings with a qafl from openings with a bayt and discuss rarer patterns.

Practical consequence: when a user requests a موشح, establish the intended structural model and preserve repeated units and the rhyme scheme. Show a compact map of those units when analyzing it. Do not repair it into ordinary monorhymed couplets or call a varied rhyme an error without checking its pattern. A short excerpt may not establish the full form.

The text includes Judat al-Rikabi's editorial introduction and manuscript variants; separate them from the author's rules. Its transcription does not justify importing every count as a universal rule for all later music labeled موشح. This pass verifies structural distinctions, not every historical weight or performance practice. The scan and metadata of the printed edition were not independently verified.

## Suggested changes to the skill

- Add a brief close-reading procedure for diction, semantic syntax, image function, sound recurrence, transitions, and the ending. Attach each recommendation to the appropriate source above in the bundled craft reference.
- Expand identification to allow a specific requested tradition or stanza form, rather than treating عمودي، تفعيلة، قصيدة نثر as exhaustive.
- During revision, explicitly check semantic and grammatical tradeoffs before and after metrical repairs.
- Make literary suggestions evidence-led: name a passage, explain its effect, and propose a bounded edit. Keep matters of taste separate from a verified technical fault.
- Offer an original prose example comparing a plain statement, an ornamental paraphrase, and an image that changes the reader's perception. Label it an exercise, not evidence of literary excellence or a historical quotation.
- Preserve analytical uncertainty for fragments and unvocalized text. Thematic similarity or familiar diction does not prove attribution, meter, or form.

## Contemporary extension: taf‘ila lineation

Muslih Abdelfattah Najjar, [التدوير في شعر التفعيلة العربي: شعر معين بسيسو أنموذجًا](https://ojs.aiou.edu.pk/index.php/jar/article/download/1357/1227/3452), Journal of Arabic Research, Allama Iqbal Open University, vol. 6, issue 1, published 2023-06-30. The university-hosted PDF identifies the author as مصلح عبد الفتاح النجار, Hashemite University. Read PDF pp. 6–8 for the terminology argument and pp. 9–11 for applications and scans of Bseiso's poems.

The study distinguishes a foot divided between lines, a word divided between lines, and more involved combinations. It argues against an absolute incompatibility between tadwir and rhyme, then analyzes examples. These classifications and that argument belong to this researcher; they are not a consensus taxonomy.

Practical consequence: inspect whether the printed break, metrical boundary, and end of the syntactic thought coincide. Scan across a break when the foot continues. Explain the effect of continuing or pausing and disclose الوصل/الوقف choices. Do not flatten the poet's lineation to avoid tadwir, assume every break resets pronunciation, or call every continuation of meaning a divided metrical foot. The source supports this procedure through Bseiso examples, not a universal license for arbitrary alterations.

## Contemporary extension: prose-poem patterns and recurrence

Isam Asal Hasan, [انماط الشكل الكتابي في قصيدة النثر انسي الحاج انموذجا](https://amm.uomustansiriyah.edu.iq/index.php/mustansiriyah/article/view/562), مجلة آداب المستنصرية, issue 83, 2018. The [full university-hosted PDF](https://amm.uomustansiriyah.edu.iq/index.php/mustansiriyah/article/download/562/510/1093) names عصام عسل حسن, Mustansiriya University. Read PDF pp. 6 and 10 on overlapping patterns and short/long works, p. 16 on repetition offering different angles, and pp. 20–21 on dialogue and patterned repetition.

The research analyzes Onsi al-Haj's compositions through tale, subject, central image, repetition, dialogue, and other patterns. It discusses combinations and limitations of classification. Its application shows recurrence developing changed meanings and contrasted states, rather than merely counting repeated words. The title's شكل كتابي concerns these compositional patterns; this is not a detailed typography or whitespace study.

Practical consequence: choose or recognize a suitable organizing device without enforcing a checklist. A prose poem can develop through a repeated phrase, central image, dialogue, or a brief scene. Ask what changes at each recurrence. Preserve intentional repetitions that structure a poem; edit redundant ones when their role is absent. Avoid defining every prose poem as short, linear, or dependent on a final surprise. These are derived review choices, not universal genre rules.
